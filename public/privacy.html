Privacy Policy

ShadowHunt Pro (web app and Android/iOS apps) · Last updated: 20 November 2026

ShadowHunt Pro is a real-world multiplayer hide-and-seek game. To make the game work it uses your device location while you are in a match. This policy explains exactly what is collected, why, who can see it and how to delete it.

We do not sell your data, show ads, use advertising IDs, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

Who can see your location

  • Your exact position is visible only to you and your own team during a match (and to a non-playing referee host, if the room uses one).
  • Opponents only ever see a blurred, scheduled reveal generated by the server — never your exact live position.
  • After a match, players of that match can view the replay track for up to 48 hours.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with our backend provider Supabase (database, sign-in, realtime). The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Notifications (if enabled) go through your browser's or phone's push service. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete location history any time: Profile → Privacy → Delete my location history now.
  • Delete your account and all data: Profile → Danger zone → Delete my account. This is immediate and permanent.
  • If you can no longer open the app, request deletion by opening an issue at github.com/jeeveshpatil/skills-github-pages/issues with your callsign (do not post personal details publicly); it will be handled within 30 days.
  • You can revoke location, notification and microphone permissions in your device settings at any time; the game stops sharing immediately.

Safety

ShadowHunt is played in the real world. Any player can trigger an emergency stop that pauses the match for everyone. Stay aware of traffic and play only in safe, public places with people you know.

Children

ShadowHunt Pro is not directed at children under 13 and should not be used by them.
